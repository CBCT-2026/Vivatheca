Pulp necrosis with chronic apical abscess in a mandibular first molar: root canal treatment or extraction下顎(かがく)第一大臼歯(だいいちだいきゅうし)の歯髄壊死(しずいえし)と慢性根尖膿瘍(まんせいこんせんのうよう)：根管治療(こんかんちりょう)か抜歯(ばっし)か

Terms:用語(ようご)： Duncan HF, et al. Treatment of pulpal and apical disease: the European Society of Endodontology (ESE) S3-level clinical practice guideline. Int Endod J. 2023.

This page is for a lower back tooth whose nerve has died, usually because of deep decay. Germs have spread from the dead nerve out of the root tips into the bone and made an abscess.1 It may not hurt much, but it will not heal on its own.

Your dentist may offer two ways to treat it. In root canal treatment, the dead nerve is cleaned out, the roots are filled and the tooth is built up again, usually with a crown. The other way is to take the tooth out, and later fill the gap or leave it.18 Here you can see the two side by side, with a 3D model for each. Use them to talk it through with your dentist. This page does not choose for you.

治療(ちりょう)の方法(ほうほう)は2つあります。1つは根(ね)の治療(ちりょう)（根管治療(こんかんちりょう)）で、死(し)んだ神経(しんけい)をきれいに取(と)り、根(ね)の中(なか)を詰(つ)めて、ふつうはかぶせ物(もの)で歯(は)を作(つく)り直(なお)します。もう1つは歯(は)を抜(ぬ)く方法(ほうほう)で、後(あと)ですき間(ま)を補(おぎな)うか、そのままにします。18ここでは2つを3Dモデルで並(なら)べました。歯医者(はいしゃ)さんと話(はな)し合(あ)うための資料(しりょう)で、どちらがよいかを決(き)めるものではありません。

Option 1: root canal treatment方法(ほうほう)1：根(ね)の治療(ちりょう)

The tooth is kept. The inside is cleaned, filled and covered.歯(は)を残(のこ)します。中(なか)をきれいにして詰(つ)め、かぶせます。

The tooth is cut in half in this model so you can see inside. Decay (dark brown) has reached the nerve space, and the nerve inside has died (dark grey). At the root tips, a green ball shows the abscess in the bone.1

この模型(もけい)では、歯(は)を半分(はんぶん)に切(き)って中(なか)が見(み)えるようにしています。虫歯(むしば)（こげ茶(ちゃ)）が神経(しんけい)の部屋(へや)まで届(とど)き、中(なか)の神経(しんけい)は死(し)んでいます（暗(くら)い灰色(はいいろ)）。根(ね)の先(さき)の緑(みどり)の玉(たま)が、骨(ほね)の中(なか)のうみのたまりです。1

A lower right first molar cut in half lengthways in its bone, seen from the cheek side. Dark decay has broken into the crown, the nerve space inside is dark grey, and a green ball of abscess sits in the bone around the root tips. 骨(ほね)の中(なか)で縦(たて)に半分(はんぶん)に切(き)った右下(みぎした)の奥歯(おくば)を、ほお側(がわ)から見(み)たところ。暗(くら)い虫歯(むしば)が頭(あたま)の部分(ぶぶん)にくい込(こ)み、中(なか)の神経(しんけい)の部屋(へや)は暗(くら)い灰色(はいいろ)で、根(ね)の先(さき)のまわりの骨(ほね)に緑(みどり)のうみの玉(たま)があります。

With the tooth numb, the dentist removes the decay and opens the top of the tooth. The dead nerve is cleaned out of the three thin root canals with fine files and a disinfecting rinse.1 This usually takes one to three visits; one visit or several give similar results.9

麻酔(ますい)をして、虫歯(むしば)を取(と)り、歯(は)の上(うえ)に穴(あな)をあけます。細(ほそ)い器具(きぐ)と消毒(しょうどく)の薬(くすり)で、3本(ぼん)の細(ほそ)い根(ね)の管(くだ)から死(し)んだ神経(しんけい)をきれいに取(と)ります。1ふつう1〜3回(かい)の通院(つういん)で、1回(かい)でも数回(すうかい)でも結果(けっか)はほぼ同(おな)じです。9

The same cut tooth during treatment. The decay and the dark nerve are gone, leaving a clean hollow through the crown and down each root. The green abscess is still in the bone. 治療(ちりょう)中(ちゅう)の同(おな)じ歯(は)。虫歯(むしば)と暗(くら)い神経(しんけい)はなくなり、頭(あたま)から根(ね)の先(さき)まできれいな空洞(くうどう)になっています。緑(みどり)のうみはまだ骨(ほね)の中(なか)にあります。

The clean canals are filled with a rubbery material (orange-pink) and the opening is sealed with a filling (blue).1 Pain is common before treatment and usually settles fast: about 81 in 100 people have pain before, 40 in 100 a day after, and 11 in 100 a week after.6

きれいになった根(ね)の管(くだ)をゴムのような材料(ざいりょう)（オレンジがかったピンク）で詰(つ)め、穴(あな)を詰(つ)め物(もの)（青(あお)）でふさぎます。1痛(いた)みは治療(ちりょう)前(まえ)に多(おお)く、ふつうすぐに落(お)ち着(つ)きます。治療(ちりょう)前(まえ)は100人(にん)中(ちゅう)約(やく)81人(にん)、1日後(にちご)は40人(にん)、1週間後(しゅうかんご)は11人(にん)に痛(いた)みがあります。6

The same cut tooth after treatment. Each root canal is filled with an orange-pink material and the hole in the crown is sealed with a blue filling. The green abscess is still there. 治療(ちりょう)後(ご)の同(おな)じ歯(は)。根(ね)の管(くだ)はオレンジがかったピンクの材料(ざいりょう)で詰(つ)まり、頭(あたま)の穴(あな)は青(あお)い詰(つ)め物(もの)でふさがれています。緑(みどり)のうみはまだあります。

A crown (grey-white) covers the tooth to stop it cracking. Back teeth with a root filling but no crown were lost about 6 times as often as those with a crown.5 Over months the abscess heals and bone grows back. About 68 to 85 in 100 treated teeth heal fully on X-ray,2 and 87 in 100 are still in place after 8 to 10 years.3

歯(は)が割(わ)れないように、かぶせ物(もの)（灰色(はいいろ)がかった白(しろ)）をします。根(ね)の治療(ちりょう)をした奥歯(おくば)でかぶせ物(もの)をしなかったものは、した歯(は)の約(やく)6倍(ばい)失(うしな)われていました。5数(すう)か月(げつ)かけてうみは治(なお)り、骨(ほね)が戻(もど)ります。治療(ちりょう)した歯(は)の100本(ぽん)中(ちゅう)約(やく)68〜85本(ほん)がレントゲンで完全(かんぜん)に治(なお)り、28〜10年後(ねんご)も87本(ほん)が残(のこ)っています。3

The same tooth months later. A grey-white crown covers the whole top of the tooth, the roots are filled, and the bone around the root tips is solid where the green abscess was. 数(すう)か月後(げつご)の同(おな)じ歯(は)。灰色(はいいろ)がかった白(しろ)いかぶせ物(もの)が歯(は)の上(うえ)全体(ぜんたい)をおおい、根(ね)は詰(つ)まり、緑(みどり)のうみがあった根(ね)の先(さき)のまわりの骨(ほね)は詰(つ)まっています。

Option 2: taking the tooth out方法(ほうほう)2：歯(は)を抜(ぬ)く

The tooth and the abscess go together. A gap is left to fill or leave.歯(は)とうみをいっしょに取(と)ります。すき間(ま)は補(おぎな)うか、そのままにします。

The same tooth as in option 1: decay into the nerve space, a dead nerve, and an abscess in the bone at the root tips.1

方法(ほうほう)1と同(おな)じ歯(は)です。虫歯(むしば)が神経(しんけい)の部屋(へや)まで届(とど)き、神経(しんけい)は死(し)に、根(ね)の先(さき)の骨(ほね)にうみのたまりがあります。1

A lower right first molar cut in half lengthways in its bone, seen from the cheek side. Dark decay has broken into the crown, the nerve space inside is dark grey, and a green ball of abscess sits in the bone around the root tips. 骨(ほね)の中(なか)で縦(たて)に半分(はんぶん)に切(き)った右下(みぎした)の奥歯(おくば)を、ほお側(がわ)から見(み)たところ。暗(くら)い虫歯(むしば)が頭(あたま)の部分(ぶぶん)にくい込(こ)み、中(なか)の神経(しんけい)の部屋(へや)は暗(くら)い灰色(はいいろ)で、根(ね)の先(さき)のまわりの骨(ほね)に緑(みどり)のうみの玉(たま)があります。

With the tooth numb, the dentist loosens it and lifts it out of its socket, roots and all. The abscess at the root tips is scraped out or drains away with it.1 This is usually done in one visit.

麻酔(ますい)をして歯(は)をゆるめ、根(ね)ごと穴(あな)から持(も)ち上(あ)げて抜(ぬ)きます。根(ね)の先(さき)のうみは、かき出(だ)すか、歯(は)といっしょに出(で)ていきます。1ふつう1回(かい)の通院(つういん)で終(お)わります。

The same jaw during the extraction. The whole decayed tooth, with its dark nerve and the green abscess still attached to the root tips, has been lifted up out of the bone, leaving an empty socket below it. 抜(ぬ)いている途中(とちゅう)の同(おな)じあご。暗(くら)い神経(しんけい)と根(ね)の先(さき)についた緑(みどり)のうみごと、虫歯(むしば)の歯(は)全体(ぜんたい)が骨(ほね)から持(も)ち上(あ)がり、下(した)には空(あ)いた穴(あな)が残(のこ)っています。

An empty socket is left in the bone and the gum. It fills with a blood clot, and you keep it clean while it heals. The neighbouring teeth now have a gap between them.7

骨(ほね)と歯(は)ぐきに空(あ)いた穴(あな)が残(のこ)ります。穴(あな)は血(ち)のかたまりで埋(う)まり、治(なお)るまで清潔(せいけつ)に保(たも)ちます。両(りょう)どなりの歯(は)の間(あいだ)にすき間(ま)ができます。7

The same jaw after the extraction. Where the molar was, there is an empty socket in the bone, with the teeth on each side standing alone. 抜(ぬ)いた後(あと)の同(おな)じあご。奥歯(おくば)があったところは骨(ほね)の中(なか)の空(あ)いた穴(あな)になり、両(りょう)どなりの歯(は)だけが立(た)っています。

Bone fills the socket over a few months, but the ridge also shrinks: on average about 3.8 mm narrower and 1.2 mm lower after 6 months.7 The gap can be filled with an implant, a bridge or a denture, or left open.8 An implant crown often takes some more months and visits.12

数(すう)か月(げつ)で骨(ほね)が穴(あな)を埋(う)めますが、骨(ほね)のふくらみはやせます。6か月後(げつご)には平均(へいきん)で幅(はば)が約(やく)3.8ミリ細(ほそ)く、高(たか)さが1.2ミリ低(ひく)くなります。7すき間(ま)はインプラント、ブリッジ、入(い)れ歯(ば)で補(おぎな)うか、そのままにします。8インプラントのかぶせ物(もの)までは、さらに数(すう)か月(げつ)と何度(なんど)かの通院(つういん)がかかることが多(おお)いです。12

The same jaw months later. The socket has filled with bone and the gum has healed over it, leaving a smooth gap between the neighbouring teeth. 数(すう)か月後(げつご)の同(おな)じあご。穴(あな)は骨(ほね)で埋(う)まり、歯(は)ぐきがその上(うえ)をおおって、両(りょう)どなりの歯(は)の間(あいだ)になめらかなすき間(ま)が残(のこ)っています。

When each option is used

Dentists usually try to keep a tooth that can still be cleaned and rebuilt. Root canal treatment is advised when enough sound tooth is left to hold a filling and a crown, and the gum around it is healthy.1 Taking the tooth out is advised when the tooth is cracked through, has too little left to rebuild, or has severe gum disease, and when treatment has failed before and cannot be redone.18

Cost and the time you can give also matter. Leaving the abscess untreated is not a safe choice: it can flare up, swell and spread.1

Questions to think about

Good decision aids help you weigh what matters most to you.10 These questions may help before you talk with your dentist.

  • How much does keeping your own tooth matter to you?
  • Can you manage several visits now, for the root filling and the crown?
  • If the tooth came out, would you want to fill the gap, and could you manage the cost and visits for that?
  • How much sound tooth is left, and what does your dentist think its chances are?

Evidence

  • ESE S3 guideline (2023): non-surgical root canal treatment is the first-line treatment for pulp necrosis with apical periodontitis in a restorable tooth; extraction when the tooth is unrestorable or periodontally hopeless.1
  • Primary root canal treatment: pooled success (strict and loose radiographic criteria) 68% to 85%.2 Tooth survival after non-surgical root canal treatment: 86% (2 to 3 years), 93% (4 to 5 years), 87% (8 to 10 years); crown placement is a main prognostic factor.3 Insurance database of 1.46 million teeth: 97% retained at 8 years.4 Endodontically treated teeth without a crown were lost about 6 times as often as crowned teeth (hazard ratio 6.0).5
  • Pain prevalence: 81% before treatment, 40% at 24 hours, 11% at 7 days, trivial by 1 month.6 Single versus multiple visits: no clear difference in radiographic success (Cochrane).9
  • Extraction: post-extraction ridge changes at 6 months, about 3.8 mm horizontal and 1.24 mm vertical loss (systematic review).7 Systematic review comparing root canal treatment with restoration, implant crowns, fixed bridges and extraction without replacement: similar long-term survival for restored root-filled teeth and implant crowns; the decision rests on restorability, periodontal status, cost and patient preference.8
  • This page follows the IPDAS ideas of presenting options, outcome probabilities with the same denominator and values clarification,10 but it has not been tested or certified as a patient decision aid.
