    Rules used for every case

    • No tooth that stays in the mouth is cut. Each bone cut runs through the socket of a tooth that the surgeon takes out during surgery. Teeth inside the removed block come out with the bone.2
    • Margins are measured, not drawn: each cut was moved until the distance from the disease to the cut reached the target in the cited guidance.
    • Teeth next to a bone cut are kept but can lose their nerve supply, so the dentist checks them later.5

    About these models

    Not yet clinically reviewed. Educational model only. It shows typical anatomy or disease, not any real patient, and is simplified and may contain errors. Not for diagnosis, treatment planning, surgical guides or use in or on the body. Full disclaimer

    Prototype. The diseases are simple shapes sized from the cited case series. Gum, palate lining and muscle are not modelled except where they are removed. The neck is not shown, although neck surgery is often part of cancer treatment. The skull model has no wisdom teeth. Where guidance gives no number, our modelling choice is stated in the case notes.
