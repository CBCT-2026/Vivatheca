What is TMD?

TMD stands for temporomandibular disorders. These are problems with the jaw joints and the muscles you chew with.3

TMD can cause pain in the jaw, the face, the temple or near the ear. The jaw may click, or be hard to open wide.13 About 6 to 9 in every 100 adults have it.1

Pain in the chewing muscles is called myalgia. Your dentist finds it by asking about your pain and by pressing gently on the muscles. You do not need an X-ray or a scan to find it.3 That is why this model shows a normal jaw: sore muscles look the same as healthy ones.

顎関節症(がくかんせつしょう)（TMD）とは

顎関節症(がくかんせつしょう)は、あごの関節(かんせつ)と、ものをかむ筋肉(きんにく)の不調(ふちょう)です。3

あご、顔(かお)、こめかみ、耳(みみ)のまわりが痛(いた)むことがあります。あごが鳴(な)ったり、口(くち)を大(おお)きく開(あ)けにくくなったりもします。13大人(おとな)の100人(にん)に6～9人(にん)ほどに見(み)られます。1

かむ筋肉(きんにく)の痛(いた)みを「筋痛(きんつう)」といいます。歯科医師(しかいし)は痛(いた)みについて話(はなし)を聞(き)き、筋肉(きんにく)をやさしく押(お)して調(しら)べます。X線(せん)写真(しゃしん)などの画像(がぞう)は必要(ひつよう)ありません。3痛(いた)む筋肉(きんにく)も見(み)た目(め)は健康(けんこう)な筋肉(きんにく)と変(か)わらないため、このモデルは正常(せいじょう)なあごを示(しめ)しています。

What helps

For TMD pain that lasts for months, a large guideline from 2023 says these help most. Learn about TMD. Look after your jaw at home. Do jaw moves and stretches. Help from a physio and a kind of talking care (CBT) also helps.1

The Japanese guideline from 2023 also says to do mouth-opening moves by yourself.2

Studies show jaw moves can ease pain and help you open wider. But most of these studies are small, so we cannot be sure how much they help.45

Tap a move above to see it on the model. Your dentist or physio will show you how far to go and how often.

Your care team

TMD care often needs more than one kind of health worker. Here is who can help, and how.

  • Dentist. Some dentists treat jaw pain as their main work. Finds out what kind of TMD you have, using a set of standard questions and checks.3 Explains TMD and shows you how to care for your jaw.1
  • Physio. Teaches you jaw moves and how to hold your head and neck. Moves the jaw with their hands. The 2023 guideline strongly supports this care.1
  • Psychologist. Gives a kind of talking care (CBT). It helps you cope with pain and stress. The 2023 guideline strongly supports it.1
  • Doctor. Can look for other causes of pain and help with medicines.1

When to get help soon

See your dentist soon if your jaw gets stuck and you cannot open your mouth wide. Also go soon if it gets stuck open and you cannot close it.3

See your dentist or doctor if the pain gets worse. Also go if it is not better after a few weeks of self-care.

あごが引(ひ)っかかって口(くち)が大(おお)きく開(あ)かないときは、早(はや)めに歯科(しか)へ行(い)ってください。開(あ)いたまま閉(と)じられないときも同(おな)じです。3

  • Simplifications: muscles are smooth bundles of straight fibres, without tendons, fascia or internal layers; they are moved with the jaw by how far each point lies along its fibre (linear blend skinning), so they stretch rather than slide. No capsule, ligaments, retrodiscal tissue, digastric or hyoid muscles, soft tissue or skin. The disc follows the condyle without rotating.
  • Movements: condylar travel at smaller openings is scaled in proportion to the incisal opening; Travers et al. found the two were not correlated.12 The tongue-up opening (20 mm, drawn) is shown as rotation only; opening is never pure rotation.14 The eminence path is taken from the BodyParts3D temporal bone and flattens after about 8 mm, which is shallower than many eminences. Side-to-side and forward movements open the teeth 1 to 2° so they clear (drawn).
  • Known mismatch: the joint space above the condyle in BodyParts3D is about 1.5 mm; Ikeda and Kawamura report 2.5 mm superior joint space in optimal joints.21 The disc keeps its cited thickness and slightly overlaps the fossa at its thickest point.
  • Muscle volumes in the model (each side): masseter about 28 cm³, temporalis about 54 cm³, lateral pterygoid about 8 cm³, medial pterygoid about 7 cm³. These were not checked against a measured volume, and the temporalis is likely too large.
