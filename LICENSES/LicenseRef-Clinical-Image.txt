Clinical images (public/images/clinical/)

Each clinical photograph, radiograph or scan on Vivatheca keeps the licence it was
published under (for example CC BY 4.0 from a journal case report). The licence,
rights holder, source link, and any changes made are recorded with the image on
the page that shows it, and in the site's image data (app/data.ts and the static
page sources). See docs/image-licensing.md for the rules used to accept an image.
